Being an Adult (Officially): The Realest First 48 Hours

18 March 2026 • 1:57 PM

I had always been an adult for a while now (or so I thought), but the government said otherwise. Yeah, they restrained me from a lot of things I wanted to do. Well, whatever — now I’m finally old enough.

So real quick, I’mma just run through the first 48 hours being 18.

By 12:00 I was still awake. It was already my day, but I didn’t even know. I was still tryna go through some stuff I had to present in church the next day. Right when I was done, I started checking all over the house looking for something to eat… uhh, nothing. Not even biscuits.

So I thought to just take groceries, yh f*** it. I mean garri… come on man, there isn’t even sugar. I just sat on the floor holding my phone..

Then I heard “HAPPY BIRTHDAY” from my older sister… sorry, older cousin. I looked at my phone — 10 minutes past 12 AM. Uhh, I just sat blank. (I don’t even remember telling her thank you or wishing her happy birthday too cos it was also her birthday and… nevermind.)

And ummmm… yh, blank and hungry. So I took the garri like that — no sugar, no salt. Then I tried to get some sleep before church cos it was already Sunday morning.

Then fast forward to Sunday morning. I woke up, did my normal morning routine and left for church. It was the earliest I’ve been to church in months. Then my parents called me on my way to church to wish me and pray for me.

Yeah, so in church the first person I actually saw was my youth president and she told me Happy Birthday. I was like, “Thanks, same to you” because it was also her birthday. Then some other people wished me too.

After that I had my presentation. It was actually to take the Sunday school. Okay, it was kind of scary at the start, you know, being the first time, but later on I felt more relaxed and I’ll say I did a little above average……

So after the church service I ate in church. Yh, it was Mother’s Day too (so the food I ate was for the celebration). Then on my way back home I took some lit pictures with my second older beautiful cousin (I hope she doesn’t see this… cos I hate to tell her she’s beautiful…).

And yeah, lest I forget, some of my crazy friends posted some crazy photos I took last year and also wished me happy birthday.

Then I went to grab some drinks with my loved ones before getting home from church , all sponsored by a church member celebrating his birthday that was a day before mine.

After all that I went home and I chatted with some of my friends that are long distance. Then one of my home guys said, “Man, you know your new age means more responsibilities.” Umm, yh sure — I don’t wanna think about this yet was the response I gave him.

Then after that I had a normal mid-day. I called my lil sis, my aunt and uncle to thank them for the gift they sent me.

Yh, that was pretty much the first 24 hours and trust me, it was the most eventful.

The next day, aside from my normal routine and reading, I spent the rest thinking about my new age, my goals, things I feel I’m not doing right and all of that.

The next day I did almost the same, just that I read more, went to campus to get some things taken care of, and then I thought of someone important — someone I would say I love… even though it’s kinda complicated. She didn’t even wish me happy birthday and I felt… (uhh nevermind). I decided I’ll have a deep talk with her when I’m chanced just to know… 😤

The end.
