Am I A Good Person

21 March 2026 • 5:50 PM

If this particular question was thrown at me randomly, it is 70% likely I say “Yes I am a good person”. But later on I know Dark Frank will definitely bring it up again… Yh Sorry you probably don’t know who dark Frank is… He is actually another me (I guess) living up in my head that is usually as brutally honest AF and also very realist and he… oops I almost got carried away with dark frank.

So back to the question I’ll say that it is not actually a question for me to answer. Cos it’s actually from every person’s point of view and different people will think different things. I mean, some people see me one way while others see me completely different… and that’s what makes the question tricky.

To some people I am the coolest, nicest or one of the best persons they have met while to some people I’m the weirdest, meanest or one of the worst persons they have met.

So people who see me as Good “are they right?” — I’ll say yes they are. Also people who see me as Bad “are they right?” — I’ll also say yes they are, Cos I Can’t forcefully or persuasively change a person’s Perspective of who I am.

After all these I’ve said, If this question was thrown to me and demanded a straight Yes or No, then I’ll say that I am a good Person.

The end.
